About

Design-trained, operations-led

I'm a results-driven Director of Operations with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Design and deep experience in the national corporate signage industry. I oversee day-to-day facility operations, strengthen client relationships, tighten workflows, raise productivity and drive annual revenue growth.

I manage complex, large-scale signage projects for nationally recognized brands and organizations, working with general contractors, architects and leading design firms across the United States and Canada. A background in shipping and logistics means I plan the whole route a sign takes, from the shop to the job site.
